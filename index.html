Zanussi

Zanussi is a company founded by Antonio Zanussi nearly a century ago—specifically in 1916—through an entrepreneurial venture that established the brand as a renowned name in the home appliance industry. It has since evolved into the company known today as "Zanussi" (often referred to as "Italian Zanussi" in reference to its parent company's country of origin). Zanussi is an Italian manufacturer of home appliances that was acquired by the Swedish company Electrolux in 1984. It is a leading brand in the European kitchen appliance and equipment sector. Since 1946, Zanussi products have been exported from Italy to markets worldwide, including Austria, Belgium, the Czech Republic, Egypt, England, Russia, Germany, and many other nations. Additionally, there are numerous service centers operating under the Zanussi name across the globe, particularly in Europe and the Middle East.
